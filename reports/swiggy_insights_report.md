# Swiggy Sales Analysis — Business Insights Report
**Period:** FY2022 – FY2025 | **Prepared by:** Akash | **Date:** October 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Swiggy Limited (NSE: SWIGGY), India's leading food delivery and quick-commerce platform, has demonstrated strong revenue growth alongside a meaningful reduction in losses over the FY2022–FY2024 period. This report synthesizes key findings across four business dimensions: Food Delivery, Instamart (Quick Commerce), Swiggy One (Subscription), and Competitive Positioning.

**Top-Line Metrics (FY2024):**
- Revenue: ₹11,634 Cr (+36% YoY)
- Net Loss: ₹1,888 Cr (↓44% from FY2023)
- Total GOV: ₹36,469 Cr
- Monthly Transacting Users: 13 Million

---

## 1. National Financial Health

### Revenue Growth Is Strong and Consistent
Swiggy's revenue has grown at a CAGR of ~43% from FY2022 to FY2024:
- FY2022: ₹5,705 Cr
- FY2023: ₹8,265 Cr (+45% YoY)
- FY2024: ₹11,634 Cr (+41% YoY)

This growth is driven by increasing take rates (18.5% → 21.8%), higher order volumes, and new revenue streams (Instamart, Dineout, advertising).

### Loss Reduction Is the Real Story
While absolute revenue growth is impressive, the more important narrative for investors and analysts is **loss reduction**:
- Net Loss peaked at ₹3,758 Cr in FY2023
- Reduced to ₹1,888 Cr in FY2024 — a 50% reduction in 2 years
- Q1 FY2027 loss: ₹791 Cr — on track for EBITDA breakeven by FY2027-28

**Implication:** Swiggy is transitioning from a "growth at all costs" phase to a "profitable growth" phase — a critical milestone for any listed company.

## 2. Food Delivery Analysis

### City Concentration Risk
The top 3 cities — Bengaluru (15%), Delhi NCR (14.5%), Mumbai (13.5%) — account for **43% of total food delivery GOV**. While this reflects high urban density and food delivery culture, it also represents concentration risk.

**Recommendation:** Continued investment in Tier 2 city expansion reduces this concentration and unlocks the next growth wave.

### Tier 2 Is the Growth Engine
| City Tier | FY2024 GOV Share | YoY Growth |
|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| Tier 1 (7 cities) | ~70% | 13-20% |
| Tier 2 (13 cities) | ~20% | 21-28% |
| Tier 3 (600+ cities) | ~10% | 28-35% |

Tier 2 cities like Jaipur (+24%), Lucknow (+26%), and Indore (+28%) are growing significantly faster than established metros. This mirrors the trajectory seen in e-commerce (Flipkart, Amazon) where Tier 2 cities became the primary growth driver after Tier 1 saturation.

### Average Order Value (AOV) Gradient
AOV varies significantly by geography:
- **Mumbai: ₹490** — Highest (premium restaurants, higher disposable income)
- **Bengaluru: ₹480** — Second (tech workforce, premium cafes)
- **Patna: ₹300** — Lowest Tier 2 city (price-sensitive market)

**Implication:** Discount strategies should be tiered by city — premium cities can sustain lower discount depth while Tier 2/3 cities may need price-led user acquisition.

## 3. Instamart Analysis

### The 29x Growth Story
Instamart's GOV trajectory is one of the most remarkable in Indian consumer tech:

| FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 |
|--------|--------|--------|
| ₹500 Cr | ₹3,000 Cr | ₹8,069 Cr |

This 29x growth in 2 years validates the quick-commerce thesis: Indian consumers in urban markets want 10-15 minute grocery delivery and are willing to pay for it.

### Dark Store Economics
- 605 dark stores across 43 cities as of FY2024
- Average dark store serves ~₹13 Cr GOV/year
- Bengaluru (95 stores), Mumbai (88), Delhi (92) are the densest markets

**Key Insight:** Dark store density correlates directly with delivery time performance. Cities with higher dark store density achieve 11-12 min delivery vs 18-20 min in sparse coverage areas.

### Is Instamart Cannibalizing Food Delivery?
The data suggests **no cannibalization, but market expansion**:
- Food delivery GOV also grew 23% in FY2024 despite Instamart scaling
- Different occasions: Instamart = grocery replenishment; Food delivery = meal occasions
- Different AOV: Instamart AOV (₹410) < Food delivery AOV (₹440) — different baskets

**Implication:** Instamart is expanding Swiggy's total addressable market, not stealing from food delivery.

## 4. Swiggy One Membership

### The Subscription Flywheel
Swiggy One (₹99/month) creates a powerful behavioral flywheel:
1. **Free delivery** → removes friction → more orders per month
2. **More orders** → higher customer lifetime value
3. **Higher LTV** → justifies lower per-order marketing spend

**Evidence:**
- Members: 4.2 orders/month vs Non-members: 1.8 orders/month
- Member AOV: ₹510 vs Non-member AOV: ₹380 (+34% premium)

### Is Swiggy One Profitable?
At ₹99/month subscription revenue, and assuming ₹60 average cost per free delivery:
- Revenue per subscriber: ₹99
- Cost of free deliveries: ₹60 (approx 2-3 free deliveries per month)
- **Net benefit: ₹39/subscriber/month** — before accounting for higher GOV upside

**Conclusion:** Swiggy One is not just a loss-leader loyalty program — it's a profitable product that also increases platform engagement.

## 5. Competitive Positioning — Swiggy vs Zomato

### The Profitability Gap
The most significant competitive development: **Zomato turned profitable in FY2024 (₹351 Cr net profit)** while Swiggy is still loss-making.

This creates a structural advantage for Zomato:
- Can invest profits into user acquisition without raising dilutive capital
- Better unit economics to sustain in a price-sensitive market
- Positive stock market sentiment (Zomato stock outperformed significantly)

### Quick Commerce Battle: Instamart vs Blinkit
| Metric | Instamart (FY2024) | Blinkit (FY2024) |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Dark Stores | 605 | 791 |
| GOV (₹ Cr) | 8,069 | ~12,000 |
| Cities | 43 | ~50 |

Blinkit is ahead in scale, but Instamart is growing faster in percentage terms and reached contribution breakeven by mid-2026. **This is the defining battleground for the next decade.**

### Market Share Trend
| Year | Swiggy | Zomato |
|------|--------|--------|
| FY2022 | 47% | 53% |
| FY2023 | 45% | 55% |
| FY2024 | 44% | 56% |

Swiggy has been losing ~1-1.5% market share per year. Reversing this trend requires:
1. Faster Tier 2 city expansion
2. More aggressive Swiggy One membership growth
3. Superior Instamart execution to retain high-frequency users

## 6. Strategic Recommendations

### For Swiggy Management:
1. **Accelerate Tier 2 expansion** — these cities have 24-28% growth vs 13-15% for Tier 1. Under-served and high ROI.
2. **Scale Swiggy One aggressively** — members order 4.2x more. Every rupee spent on member acquisition pays back quickly.
3. **Close the dark store gap with Blinkit** — Quick commerce will define the winner. Swiggy needs 800+ stores by end of FY2025.
4. **Optimize take rates carefully** — at 21.8%, any further increase risks restaurant partner churn.
5. **Path to breakeven by FY2027** — the loss trend (₹3,758 Cr → ₹1,888 Cr → ₹791 Cr quarterly run-rate) is on track.

---

## 7. Conclusion

Swiggy is navigating a critical inflection point: transitioning from a high-growth, high-burn startup to a scale-efficient, listed company. The FY2024 data suggests strong fundamentals — revenue growth, loss reduction, Instamart explosion, and Swiggy One traction.

The key risk: Zomato's profitability advantage. Swiggy must reach EBITDA breakeven before Zomato's financial strength creates a winner-takes-all dynamic.

**Investment Thesis (Educational):** Swiggy is a **high-risk, high-reward** bet on India's food-tech and quick-commerce future. At the right price, the Instamart optionality alone could justify the valuation.
