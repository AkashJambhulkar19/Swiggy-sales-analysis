🔍 Key Findings — National Overview

  • Revenue grew 2.04x from ₹5,705 Cr (FY2022) to ₹11,634 Cr (FY2024), showing consistent 30%+ annual growth.
  • Net losses peaked at ₹3,758 Cr in FY2023 but were slashed to ₹1,888 Cr in FY2024 — a 50% improvement in 2 years.
  • Total GOV crossed ₹36,000 Cr in FY2024, validating Swiggy's scale in the Indian food-tech market.
  • Instamart GOV grew from ₹500 Cr (FY2022) to ₹8,069 Cr (FY2024) — a 29x surge in just 2 years.
  • Q1 FY2027 shows losses narrowing to ₹791 Cr, suggesting a clear path toward EBITDA breakeven by FY2027-28.

Food Delivery — City-wise & National Analysis

🔍 Key Findings — Food Delivery

  • Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, and Mumbai together contribute ~43% of total food delivery GOV.
  • Mumbai has the highest AOV (₹490) while Tier 2 cities like Patna trail at ₹300 — showing urban premium pricing.
  • Tier 2 cities (Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore) show 24-28% YoY growth, outpacing Tier 1 (~15%) — the next growth engine.
  • Hyderabad has the fastest food delivery time among Tier 1 cities at ~30 min average.

Instamart — Quick Commerce Analysis

🔍 Key Findings — Instamart

  • Instamart's GOV rocketed from ₹500 Cr (FY2022) to ₹8,069 Cr (FY2024) — a 29x surge in 2 years.
  • Bengaluru leads with 95 dark stores and ~20% of national Instamart GOV — the most mature quick-commerce market.
  • Average delivery time across cities hovers at 11-14 minutes in Tier 1 — well within the 15-minute promise.
  • By Q1 FY2027, Instamart GOV of ₹7,907 Cr is nearly equal to Food Delivery per quarter — a structural shift in demand.
  • Instamart reached contribution breakeven by mid-2026, proving the quick commerce unit economics model.

City Tier Analysis — Tier 1 vs Tier 2 vs Tier 3

🔍 Key Findings — City Tiers

  • Tier 1 cities (7 cities) contribute ~70% of total food delivery GOV, but this share is slowly declining as Tier 2 grows.
  • Tier 2 cities grew their GOV contribution from 18% (FY2022) to 22% (FY2024) — a key strategic opportunity.
  • Tier 3 expansion is still early, but cities like Guwahati and Bhubaneswar show 30-35% YoY growth — highest of all tiers.
  • Instamart is primarily a Tier 1 + select Tier 2 business, with Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR alone accounting for 56% of IM GOV.

Swiggy One — Subscription & Membership Analysis

🔍 Key Findings — Swiggy One

  • Swiggy One members place 4.2x more orders per month than non-members — showing strong retention uplift.
  • Member AOV (₹510) is 34% higher than non-member AOV (₹380) — members order more premium items with free delivery.
  • The subscription program creates a virtuous cycle: free delivery → more orders → higher GOV → better take rate recovery.
  • At ₹99/month, Swiggy One generates subscription revenue that covers free delivery costs with a margin — it's a profitable product.

Competitive Analysis — Swiggy vs Zomato

🔍 Key Findings — Swiggy vs Zomato

  • Zomato crossed profitability in FY2024 (₹351 Cr profit) while Swiggy is still loss-making — a key competitive distinction.
  • Zomato's Blinkit scaled faster in quick commerce: 791 dark stores vs Swiggy's 605 by end of FY2024.
  • Swiggy holds ~44% food delivery market share in FY2024 but faces pressure from Zomato's 56% — a gap that requires strong Tier 2 execution.
  • Swiggy's total GOV per user is higher — suggesting a more premium or loyal user base despite fewer MAUs.
  • Both companies are converging in quick commerce — Instamart vs Blinkit will likely define the winner for the next decade.
